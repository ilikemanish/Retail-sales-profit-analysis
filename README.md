## 📌 Project Overview

Retail businesses need to understand which regions, products, customer segments, and time periods contribute most to revenue and profit. This project analyzes retail transaction data to evaluate sales performance, profitability, customer segments, product categories, regions, order priorities, and monthly trends.

### 🎯 Key Questions Answered:
- Which region generates the highest sales?
- Which product category performs best?
- Which customer segment generates the most profit?
- Which products contribute the most revenue?
- How do sales fluctuate month by month?
- Which order priorities generate the highest sales?

## 💡 Key Insights & Recommendations

<details>
<summary><b>🔍 View Deep Data Insights</b> <i>(Click to expand)</i></summary>
<br>

- 🌎 **West** is the top-performing region by sales.
- 💻 **Technology** is the leading product category by sales.
- 👥 **Corporate** customers generate the highest profit.
- 📅 **April** records the highest monthly sales.
- 📦 **Low-priority orders** generate the highest sales among order priorities.
- 💰 The business generates strong overall sales and positive profitability.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>💼 View Strategic Recommendations</b> <i>(Click to expand)</i></summary>
<br>

- **Targeted Expansion:** Focus marketing and inventory budgets on high-performing regions (West) and product categories (Technology).
- **Client Retention:** Strengthen relationships and offer dedicated account management for profitable Corporate customer segments.
- **Trend Capitalization:** Monitor monthly sales trends to identify seasonal opportunities, specifically maximizing inventory for spring (April).
- **Logistics Audit:** Review discount structures and shipping costs to improve overall profitability without sacrificing volume.
</details>
